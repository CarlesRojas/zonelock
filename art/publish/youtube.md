Description:

Zone Lock is a Minecraft mod for Fabric and NeoForge (26.3). Seal an area with an Amethyst Core and nothing inside can be broken, built or skipped until the challenge is beaten.

How it works
• Place an Amethyst Core in your dungeon and shape its zone with the Resonance Wand.
• Seal it with an amethyst shard: the zone locks as soon as everyone has left it.
• Inside, no block can be broken or placed, no ender pearl lands, no elytra flies.
• To switch the core off, put out every seal first: by touch, or with redstone.
• Seals and cores give a redstone signal when they open, so puzzles can chain room after room.
• Hold the wand to see every locked zone.

Build puzzles, parkour and mazes that can't be skipped, or find the Amethyst Tower in the wild.
